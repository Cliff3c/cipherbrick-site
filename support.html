Help & Support

Support Center

CipherBrick Pro is a solo-developed project. Response times reflect that — but every message is read.

Before You Write

Most questions are answered in the built-in help documentation. If you are using the web app or Android app, tap the ? icon or go to Settings → Help & Documentation.

Quick answers to the most common questions:
  • Forgot your key or salt? There is no recovery. Keys and salts are never sent to a server — only you have them.
  • Can't decrypt a message? The key, salt, and mode (Standard vs Simple) must match exactly what was used to encrypt.
  • Audio not working? Bring devices within 1 meter, reduce background noise, and switch to Normal protocol in Settings → Audio.
  • HKPM requires Chrome or Edge. Firefox does not support the WebAuthn PRF extension used by hardware key mode.
  • App works offline. After the first load, no internet connection is required for any feature.

Contact Support

For questions, feature requests, or anything not covered by the help docs, reach out directly.

cliff@cipherbrick.com

Please include: what you were trying to do, what happened, your browser or app version, and your operating system. This helps resolve issues faster.

Response time is typically within a few business days. CipherBrick Pro is a solo project — there is no support team, but every message is read and taken seriously.

Security Reports

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please do not open a public GitHub issue. Report it privately by email so it can be assessed and addressed before public disclosure.

Security reports: cliff@cipherbrick.com
Please include a description of the issue and steps to reproduce.

Responsible disclosure is appreciated. There is no formal bug bounty program, but all genuine security reports are taken seriously and acknowledged.

Google Play — Refunds & Purchases

The Android app is available as a one-time paid purchase on Google Play. Google handles all payment processing and refunds.

  • Refunds within 48 hours of purchase can be requested directly through Google Play
  • For refund issues outside that window, contact Google Play support
  • If you have a question about app functionality before purchasing, the full-featured PWA is available free at app.cipherbrick.com

The Play Store purchase funds hosting, domain costs, and continued development. It does not unlock additional features — the app is identical to the free PWA.

Privacy & Data

CipherBrick Pro has no accounts, no servers, and no data collection. If you have a privacy question, the answer is almost certainly covered in the Privacy Policy. The short version:

Your keys, messages, and encrypted output never leave your device.
There is nothing stored on our end — because there is no "our end."
